# Phthonos

[繁體中文](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · **English**

> **Forge your legend, your way.**

Phthonos is a Terraria content mod in development, with the long-term ambition of creating an expansive adventure. Through combat that rewards observation and timing, alongside varied combinations of weapons, equipment, and builds, it aims to let players find their own path through its challenges.

## Read the attack. Seize the moment.

Observe enemy movements, recognize incoming attacks, and decide when to attack, dodge, or defend. Learn when to strike and how to exploit openings, making each encounter an opportunity to understand your opponent and sharpen your skills.

## Experiment with equipment. Develop your own build.

Combine weapons and equipment to suit your preferences and explore what different combinations can offer. Each build is intended to have its own strengths, limitations, and rhythm, giving you different ways to approach the same challenge and develop a personal fighting style.

## Consistent rules. Meaningful choices.

Phthonos is built around freedom within a coherent set of rules. Choices come with trade-offs, and power comes at a cost. Understanding those rules, making thoughtful equipment choices, and mastering your actions will help you overcome challenges.

## Development status

Phthonos is still in active development and design. The description above presents the project's intended direction, not a list of completed features. Content will be introduced gradually across future versions.

Systems currently being implemented and refined include:

- Player actions: rolling and crouching.
- Main-hand and off-hand weapon controls.
- Equipment display, ready stances, and weapon placement.
- Stamina consumption for rolling, passive recovery, and a stamina HUD.

These systems are still being tested and adjusted. Values, rules, and presentation may change.

## Development environment

This project uses C# and tModLoader. To test from source, place the project in tModLoader's `ModSources/Phthonos` directory, then build and reload it through the in-game Mod Sources interface.

The stamina HUD currently supports the vanilla horizontal resource bar styles. Select a horizontal bar style in Settings when testing.
